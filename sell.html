Sawal-jawab

Graspion par business jodne ka kitna kharcha hai?

Joining aur product listing free hai. Koi mahine ki fees nahi. Sirf saaman bikne par category ke hisaab se chhota commission lagta hai.

Kya GST zaroori hai?

Zyadatar products ke liye GST registration chahiye. Kuch chhote vendor ke liye niyam alag ho sakte hain — form bharne par hamari team call karke saaf bata degi.

Delivery kaun karega?

Lucknow mein aapke area ke grahak ko Routix rider jaldi pahunchata hai. Baaki India mein courier (Shiprocket) se jaata hai — label aapke vendor panel se banta hai.

Paisa kab aur kaise milega?

Order deliver hone aur return ka samay poora hone ke baad paisa seedha aapke bank khate mein bheja jaata hai. Har order ka hisaab vendor panel mein dikhta hai.

Kya-kya bech sakte hain?

Kirana, mithai, ghee-tel, kapde, electronics, office supplies, handicraft, FPO aur ghar ke bane products — jo kanoon ke andar ho.
