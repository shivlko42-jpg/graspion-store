🛡️ Parcel Suraksha

Routix ki guarantee — aapka parcel, hamari zimmedari.
What it is: Parcel Suraksha is Routix's own guarantee for your parcel. It is not an insurance policy from an insurance company. For ₹10 per parcel, if your parcel is lost or damaged while Routix has it, Routix pays you back as per these rules.

What is covered

  • Parcel lost after the rider picked it up
  • Parcel damaged during the trip because of the rider or Routix
  • An item missing from a sealed parcel
  • Up to the value you declared at booking, maximum ₹5,000 per parcel

What is not covered

  • Weak or poor packing (glass, liquids, food not packed properly)
  • Things that spoil on their own (fresh food, flowers) or were already damaged
  • Cash, jewellery, documents of value, or anything we don't carry
  • Delays, or anything after the receiver signed off with the OTP and found the parcel fine
  • A value higher than what you declared

How to claim

  • Open your tracking link → Report a problem, within 24 hours of delivery (or as soon as you know it is lost)
  • Tell us what happened and the amount
  • Send photos of the parcel and the bill / proof of value on WhatsApp 80048 73819 with your booking number
  • We check the rider's delivery photo and details and reply within 3 working days
  • If approved, we pay to your UPI / bank within 7 working days

Good to know

  • One claim per parcel. Our decision is based on photos, the rider's report and your proof.
  • Routix may change these rules for new bookings; the rules on the day you booked apply.
