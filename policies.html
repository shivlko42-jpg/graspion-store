🏢 About Us

Graspion is operated by Grasp Infotech, based in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, India.

We give local vendors the opportunity to start selling online without heavy upfront investment, and give customers a quick-delivery experience powered by trusted local sellers.

🔒 Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data.

1. What Data We Collect

  • Name, phone number, and delivery address (at checkout)
  • Order history and payment status (payment details are handled directly by Razorpay; we do not store card/UPI details)
  • Location/pincode (for delivery purposes)

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide customer support

Your data is shared only with the relevant vendor and delivery partner to fulfil your order. We do not sell your data to third-party marketing companies.

You may request to view, update, or delete your data by contacting us at graspion@gmail.com.

🚚 Shipping Policy

Delivery Areas

Graspion currently delivers to select pincodes. Please check your pincode before placing an order to confirm delivery availability.

Delivery Time

Most local orders are delivered within 10–30 minutes, depending on vendor location and order volume. Products shipped Pan-India may take 3–7 business days.

Delivery Charges

Delivery is FREE for orders above ₹199. A delivery fee of ₹29 applies to orders below this amount.

In Case of Delay

If your delivery is running later than expected, please check the order tracking page or contact our support team.

↩️ Cancellation & Refund Policy

Order Cancellation

Orders can be cancelled before they move to "Preparing" status. Once a vendor has packed or dispatched an order, cancellation is no longer possible.

Refund Eligibility

  • Order cancelled before dispatch — full refund
  • Product received damaged or incorrect — replacement or refund, subject to vendor policy
  • Order not delivered due to vendor/delivery issue — full refund

Refund Timeline

Approved refunds are credited to your original payment method within 5–7 business days via Razorpay.

Non-Refundable Cases

If a product was delivered in good condition and a return is requested without valid reason, the refund will be at the vendor's discretion. All returns require a valid reason and proof (such as a photo) and are subject to manual review before approval — refunds are never processed automatically.

💰 Pricing Details

Each product's price is clearly displayed on its page, inclusive of applicable GST/tax unless stated otherwise.

Additional Charges

  • Delivery Fee: ₹29 for orders under ₹199; FREE for orders ₹199 and above
  • Platform Fee: None currently charged
  • GST: Included in the listed product price, based on the vendor's HSN classification

Payment Methods

We accept UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, and Cash on Delivery (COD, in select areas) — all payments are processed securely through Razorpay.
